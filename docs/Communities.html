Communities & Guides

Discord Communities:​

  • ModdingLinked - For Viva New Vegas, The Best Of Times and more.
  • Tale of Two Wastelands - For the Tale of Two Wastelands mod.
  • The Wasteland Supplemental - For WSG, SALVO and H2O.
  • xNVSE - For NVSE and its plugins, also great for modding info.
  • Another Millenia Stuff - For the Another Millenia mod and more
  • NH Arms - Mostly known for ported assets and non-nexus mods.
  • TES Reloaded - Mainly useful for New Vegas Reloaded.
  • Sweet Retreat - Sweet6Shooter's discord.

Guides:​

  • Viva New Vegas - A crucial modding guide to create a stable working game and modding environment for New Vegas.
  • Wasteland Survival Guide - A quality modding guide for TTW.
  • New Vegas Visual Renewal - A newly revised, refreshed, and more complete visual update guide for Fallout New Vegas and Tale of Two Wastelands.
  • FNV Performance Guide - Wall's based performance guide that everyone should follow.
  • Decompression Guide - A guide on how to easily remove the compression flag from plugins to improve performance.
  • LOD Guide - A comprehensive guide on how to generate Object and Terrain LOD with xLODgen.
  • Bits of NifSkopery - A collection of notes about the Static or Havoked nif structure and manipulating nif file settings in NifSkope.

Info:​

  • xEdit - Introduction to xEdit, which will allow you to understand how plugins work and how you can edit records.
  • xEdit Video - A good video by cloudisms that covers multiple parts of xEdit.
  • The Method - Guide on how to use The Method, which is used while creating a modlist in order to patch it properly and hide remaining conflicts that need no intervention.
  • Safe Modding - List of many outdated/dangerous mods and tools.
  • GECK Wiki - Community continuation of Bethesda's GECK wiki, maintained by NVSE modders; focused on GECK, scripting and game's inner workings.
  • Independent Fallout Wiki - Fan project focused on maintaining a high-quality, comprehensive and accessible encyclopedia about all aspects of the Fallout games, community and mods.
