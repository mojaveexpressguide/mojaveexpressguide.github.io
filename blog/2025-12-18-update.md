**REMOVED:**

- Enhanced Mojave Plus - Searchlight - It's been replaced by the Revised Mojave update
- DM's Gun Runners Gone Shootin - Although cool it has an unnecessarily large bsa, left over forms and out of place magazines.
- Functional Post Game Ending - It's just underwhelming. The game ends at hoover dam which you will never reach anyway.
- Quick Select - The mod is incompatible with B42 HOT BNB and the vanilla system is fine 
- Quick Select Throwables - Necessary as the above has been removed.
- Walking Intertia - Gets annoying and does not work nicely with enhanced creatures.
- Subtle Camera Shake (NVSE) replaced by Dynamic Camera Shake
- Fixed NPC Karma Alignments - Weird balancing and karma is just weak in this game anyway.
- MCM BugFix 2 - Redundant with MCM Extender.
- Faction Disguise De-Scripter - No longer required due to EVEM update
- Pipboy Holo Panel ESPless Rebuild Patch - No longer needed with latest Holo Panel update.
- Pip-Boy Screen Always On (No Plugin) - Issues with Realtime PipBoy Map - ESPless
- Legion Armor Revamp Project 2.0 - Replaced by Legion Armor Reflects Player Strength
